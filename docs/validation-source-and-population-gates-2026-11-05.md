# Validation source and population gates — 2026-11-05

The validation engine previously accepted a BuildSpec and population projection but did not inspect them. It now recomputes the BuildSpec content hash and checks the workbook's BuildSpec, case and architecture identities against it, along with the supplied approved population decision, profile hash and BuildSpec population lineage. A mismatch adds a blocking finding and produces a freshly hashed validation result. These checks establish consistency only; they do not authenticate the asserted decision or prove the profile bytes and original evidence are authoritative.

Structural population validation now blocks every I/B mapping until participant-row routing can be independently verified. A present value, including zero, does not establish its source. This closes a separate route by which the preliminary model builder's null or unbound inputs could have received a passing structural validation. I-cell classification checks now include the sheet name in physical cell identity. Constant-formula synthetic models without I/B mappings can still pass structural validation; they are not evidence of actuarial or release readiness.
