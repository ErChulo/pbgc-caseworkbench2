Hashes and locally entered reviewer names do not prove authority or independent validation.

5. **Release decision and evidence:** pass the complete unit/integration/contract and applicable browser/office acceptance suites with approved dependencies and real authorized reference data, close material defects, preserve the test evidence without committing sensitive data, and obtain the required human approvals.
