# Formula approval evidence blocker

The wizard previously created `TEST-<cell>` and `ORACLE-<cell>` identifiers automatically for every formula approval, without establishing that those references existed. It also silently assigned a locally typed reviewer an `actuary` authority context. These values were not proof of a test, oracle, reviewer authority or external calculation. The wizard's formula-approval action is now blocked until governed test and oracle references can be selected and verified; the interface states the blocker and does not create placeholder identifiers. Domain-level synthetic tests and approval replay remain available for development, but they do not establish production approval.

This supersedes the earlier browser milestone's statement that the wizard recorded four synthetic formula decisions. The browser test now confirms the formula controls are blocked, tests changed source bytes and review invalidation, and verifies no workbook download is offered. Scenario collision and I/B routing blockers remain independently necessary. A future implementation needs real case-scoped evidence selection, authority verification, and decision persistence; it must not simply generate names or claim external execution.
